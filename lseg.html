01 OVERVIEW

The London Stock Exchange Group (LSEG) employee portal is the central hub for employees to access HR services, company policies, internal tools and more throughout their journey.

Over the years, the portal became cluttered with information, making it difficult for employees to find what they needed quickly.

Project Goal

Modernize the portal experience by simplifying navigation, improving information hierarchy, and surfacing important actions while preserving familiar workflows.

02 THE CHALLENGE

Information Overload

Too much information on the homepage created cognitive overload and confusion.

Difficult Navigation

Important features were hard to locate due to poor grouping.

Outdated Interface

The visual design was dated and did not align with modern UX standards.

Low Discoverability

Frequently used actions were buried among low-priority content.

03 RESEARCH

I conducted interviews with employees across departments and stakeholders from HR and IT teams to understand pain points and expectations.

Research Methods

  • ✓ 8 Employee Interviews
  • ✓ 3 Stakeholder Interviews
  • ✓ Heuristic Evaluation
  • ✓ Competitive Analysis

Key Questions

  • Which features do you use most frequently?
  • What tasks take the longest to complete?
  • What is difficult to find or understand?
  • What would you change to improve your experience?

04 KEY FINDINGS

Employees use a small set of features frequently

Most users repeatedly accessed 5–6 core features on a daily or weekly basis.

Homepage lacked hierarchy

All modules competed for attention, making it difficult to prioritize tasks.

Navigation was inconsistent

Different sections followed different patterns, causing confusion.

Users wanted a cleaner, modern look

Employees expected a modern, simple and easy to scan interface.

05 DESIGN DECISIONS

Prioritize Quick Actions

Surfaced the most frequently used actions on the homepage.

Use Card-Based Layout

Grouped content in cards to create hierarchy and improve scannability.

Consistent Navigation

Standardized labels and patterns across the portal.

Progressive Disclosure

Moved secondary content into sections to reduce cognitive load.

Modern Visual Language

Introduced a clean, modern design system with better accessibility.

11 OUTCOMES

Simplified experience

Better discoverability

Users can find features with fewer clicks.

Modern & engaging UI

Positive feedback

Employees loved the simpler, organized experience.

12 REFLECTION

This project taught me the importance of balancing innovation with familiarity in enterprise products. Understanding user behavior and prioritizing their daily tasks helped me create a portal that is both modern and easy to use. Given more time, I would conduct usability testing post-launch and measure task completion time to quantify the impact.

What I'd improve next

  • Conduct usability testing post-launch
  • Measure task completion time and success rate
  • Personalization based on user roles
