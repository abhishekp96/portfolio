01. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Pashushala is a comprehensive cattle management platform designed to help farmers and dairy owners manage their daily operations efficiently. The platform enables users to track animal profiles, health records, breeding details, milk production, feed, and inventory in one place.

The goal of this project was to create a simple, mobile-friendly solution that brings all farm activities together and improves decision making.

02. THE CHALLENGE

Farmers were using manual methods and scattered records, which led to inefficiencies and data loss.

  • Difficult to track animal health and treatments
  • No centralized records for breeding & production
  • Manual calculations and paper based logs
  • Lack of reminders for important tasks
  • Time-consuming reporting and insights
  • Limited visibility into overall farm performance

07. CONSTRAINTS

  • Limited technical resources
  • Budget and timeline constraints
  • Need to support both desktop and mobile
  • Works in low connectivity areas
  • Data security and privacy

06. KEY FINDINGS

  • Farmers spend excessive time maintaining paper records
  • Health records and vaccination schedules are often missed
  • Breeding and production data is not easily accessible
  • Need for simple dashboards and quick insights
  • Mobile access is essential for on-field usage

Save Time
